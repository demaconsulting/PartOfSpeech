# Confidence Thresholds (Development Selection)

Generated by the `thresholds` command of the trainer from the two development sets. It reads no held-out file. The operating points below were chosen by the rule that was pre-registered in `tools/DemaConsulting.PartOfSpeech.Trainer/RUN-LOG.md` before this report existed, and are the only thresholds that the held-out evaluation applies.

This record belongs to the sixth held-out evaluation of the model family: the held-out sets were already seen for the first retrained model, again for the model that followed it, again for the model with the clause-cue feature group, again for the model with the imperative training source, again for the model with the two compound training sources (and for the model before the label corrections, so with this one seven models in all are scored on them), so this evaluation is a disclosed sixth look, not a first look at unseen data. The model was selected on development data only, by a rule that was fixed before any training (the section "beta.3 phase 2 (E2): pre-registered technical-mix experiment" of [`RUN-LOG.md`](../../tools/DemaConsulting.PartOfSpeech.Trainer/RUN-LOG.md)), and the registration of that experiment provided for this one evaluation if a candidate shipped; nothing was selected, tuned or changed on its result, and the previous model is shown beside it (see the [evaluation overview](README.md)).

## Model and data

- Model: uncompressed image SHA-256 `42352bed32845de78380dcda387aac3915468ed9cf4929e8571b95486f7e245b`, softmax temperature 9.514.
- Open development split: 26,232 tokens, 4,451 noun-verb decisions, accuracy 96.69%.
- Gold v2 development split without `to` and `not`: 2,017 targets, 611 noun-verb decisions, accuracy 95.24%.
- Pooled: 5,062 noun-verb decisions.
- At the harmful limit 0.45 the harmful errors are 92 (open), 8 (gold v2) and 100 (pooled), the figures of the frozen model in the run log.

A decision is a token whose gold tag is a noun or a verb and for which both were candidates. A decision is answered at a threshold t when the confidence of the chosen tag is at least t. A harmful error is an answered decision where the noun was tagged as a verb or the reverse. Coverage is the share of the decisions that is answered.

## Selection rule `nv-pooled-dev-v1`

On the pooled development decisions the thresholds of the grid (0.45 to 0.95 in steps of 0.05, then 0.96 to 0.99) are scanned in ascending order and the **first** one that meets both the harmful-error target and the coverage floor is the operating point. If none does, the point is none; the targets are not relaxed afterwards.

| Point | Harmful per 1,000 answered at most | Coverage at least (%) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| strict | 5.0 | 60 |
| balanced | 10.0 | 85 |

- The figures are development figures: the tranches are the data the model was compared on, so they are optimistic for any other text.
- The tranches are band-selected contested words in technical sentences (deverbal heads, defined terms, step lines, clause verbs), not running text, so the rates are not the error rates on ordinary text.
- No held-out set carries these shapes, and the held-out evaluation applies the general operating points only.
- The measure counts every wrong answer of a judged word (targets and controls) under the scoring of its tranche, not the harmful NOUN<->VERB errors of the general operating points; the harmful rate is reported beside it.
Reading of the point (written by hand, not generated): it rests on 357 answers with one wrong answer. It clears the coverage floor of 25% by 2 answers (355 of the 1,417 judged words are needed), its 95% interval reaches 15.7 wrong per 1,000 answers (above the target of 10), and the next threshold below it, 0.98, has 13.2 wrong per 1,000 answers (6 of 454), above the target. It is an estimate with a wide interval, not a guarantee of 1%.
