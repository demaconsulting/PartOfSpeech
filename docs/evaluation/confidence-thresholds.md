# Confidence Thresholds (Development Selection)

Generated by the `thresholds` command of the trainer from the two development sets. It reads no held-out file. The operating points below were chosen by the rule that was pre-registered in `tools/DemaConsulting.PartOfSpeech.Trainer/RUN-LOG.md` before this report existed, and are the only thresholds that the held-out evaluation applies.

This record belongs to the third held-out evaluation of the model family: the held-out sets were already seen for the first retrained model and again for the model that followed it (and for the model before the label corrections, so four models in all have been scored on them), so this evaluation is a disclosed third look, not a first look at unseen data. The model was selected on development data only, by a rule that was fixed before any training (the section "Context-feature experiment" of [`RUN-LOG.md`](../../tools/DemaConsulting.PartOfSpeech.Trainer/RUN-LOG.md)), and the owner authorized this one evaluation; nothing was selected, tuned or changed on its result, and the previous model is shown beside it (see the [evaluation overview](README.md)).

## Model and data

- Model: uncompressed image SHA-256 `ec3f090eb318abc1bcf4dc8a88e401567e606932142178277d3ec387e0aa40f3`, softmax temperature 9.514.
- Open development split: 26,232 tokens, 4,451 noun-verb decisions, accuracy 96.65%.
- Gold v2 development split without `to` and `not`: 2,017 targets, 611 noun-verb decisions, accuracy 94.84%.
- Pooled: 5,062 noun-verb decisions.
- At the harmful limit 0.45 the harmful errors are 100 (open), 16 (gold v2) and 116 (pooled), the figures of the frozen model in the run log.

A decision is a token whose gold tag is a noun or a verb and for which both were candidates. A decision is answered at a threshold t when the confidence of the chosen tag is at least t. A harmful error is an answered decision where the noun was tagged as a verb or the reverse. Coverage is the share of the decisions that is answered.

## Selection rule `nv-pooled-dev-v1`

On the pooled development decisions the thresholds of the grid (0.45 to 0.95 in steps of 0.05, then 0.96 to 0.99) are scanned in ascending order and the **first** one that meets both the harmful-error target and the coverage floor is the operating point. If none does, the point is none; the targets are not relaxed afterwards.

| Point | Harmful per 1,000 answered at most | Coverage at least (%) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| strict | 5.0 | 60 |
| balanced | 10.0 | 85 |

A threshold chosen on development data is optimistic; the held-out evaluation reports what it does on data that were not used to choose it.
