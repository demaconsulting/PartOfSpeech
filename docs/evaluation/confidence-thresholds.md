# Confidence Thresholds (Development Selection)

Generated by the `thresholds` command of the trainer from the two development sets. It reads no held-out file. The operating points below were chosen by the rule that was pre-registered in `tools/DemaConsulting.PartOfSpeech.Trainer/RUN-LOG.md` before this report existed, and are the only thresholds that the held-out evaluation applies.

This record belongs to the fourth held-out evaluation of the model family: the held-out sets were already seen for the first retrained model, again for the model that followed it and again for the model with the clause-cue feature group (and for the model before the label corrections, so five models in all have been scored on them), so this evaluation is a disclosed fourth look, not a first look at unseen data. The model was selected on development data only, by a rule that was fixed before any training (the section "Imperative training data" of [`RUN-LOG.md`](../../tools/DemaConsulting.PartOfSpeech.Trainer/RUN-LOG.md)), and the registration of that experiment provided for this one evaluation if a candidate shipped; nothing was selected, tuned or changed on its result, and the previous model is shown beside it (see the [evaluation overview](README.md)).

## Model and data

- Model: uncompressed image SHA-256 `6e1e4ade56aa277d79709c107000f10e99c6f4e55a097e872a9c38fa2e12e8d0`, softmax temperature 9.514.
- Open development split: 26,232 tokens, 4,451 noun-verb decisions, accuracy 96.57%.
- Gold v2 development split without `to` and `not`: 2,017 targets, 611 noun-verb decisions, accuracy 95.04%.
- Pooled: 5,062 noun-verb decisions.
- At the harmful limit 0.45 the harmful errors are 91 (open), 14 (gold v2) and 105 (pooled), the figures of the frozen model in the run log.

A decision is a token whose gold tag is a noun or a verb and for which both were candidates. A decision is answered at a threshold t when the confidence of the chosen tag is at least t. A harmful error is an answered decision where the noun was tagged as a verb or the reverse. Coverage is the share of the decisions that is answered.

## Selection rule `nv-pooled-dev-v1`

On the pooled development decisions the thresholds of the grid (0.45 to 0.95 in steps of 0.05, then 0.96 to 0.99) are scanned in ascending order and the **first** one that meets both the harmful-error target and the coverage floor is the operating point. If none does, the point is none; the targets are not relaxed afterwards.

| Point | Harmful per 1,000 answered at most | Coverage at least (%) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| strict | 5.0 | 60 |
| balanced | 10.0 | 85 |

A threshold chosen on development data is optimistic; the held-out evaluation reports what it does on data that were not used to choose it.
